## TEAO Concludes a Successful Exhibition in Shanghai

From **August 13 to 15, 2025**, TEAO participated in the Shanghai International Automotive Interior & Exterior Exhibition, presenting our latest damper technologies and motion-control solutions for automotive interior and exterior applications.

During the three-day exhibition, the TEAO booth welcomed a steady flow of visitors, including OEM representatives, Tier-1 suppliers, design teams, engineering professionals and industry experts. The event gave us a valuable opportunity to demonstrate our product capabilities, exchange technical insights and explore new cooperation opportunities with partners across the automotive supply chain.

## Showcasing Motion-Control Solutions for Automotive Applications

At the exhibition, TEAO focused on practical damper solutions for automotive interior and exterior mechanisms, including rotary dampers, axial dampers, synchronizer mechanisms and glove box dampers. These products are designed to help automotive components move more smoothly, reduce operating noise and improve the overall user experience.

TEAO also presented its capabilities in precision manufacturing, process control and application-oriented product development. For automotive customers, stable damping performance is not only about product function. It also affects perceived quality, cabin quietness, mechanism reliability and long-term user satisfaction.

## Technical Exchange with Industry Partners

Throughout the exhibition, many visitors discussed product performance, application structures, torque requirements and specific vehicle-interior use cases with the TEAO team. These conversations were highly valuable because they connected real customer requirements with TEAO's engineering experience in damper design and production.

The discussions covered both current applications and future trends, including quieter vehicle interiors, more refined opening and closing mechanisms, personalized cabin experiences and the growing demand for reliable compact motion-control components in new vehicle platforms.

## Strong Interest and New Cooperation Opportunities

One of TEAO's main goals for this exhibition was to build deeper connections with customers and industry partners. Over the three days, we met hundreds of professional visitors and received many project inquiries from vehicle manufacturers, established component suppliers and innovative companies working on new automotive mechanisms.

Many customers showed strong interest in TEAO's damper products and engineering support. Several discussions moved beyond general introduction into project-specific topics, including application structure, sample evaluation, torque matching and future cooperation plans.
