TEAO Privacy Policy
Last updated: May 22, 2026

This policy explains how TEAO handles information submitted through website inquiry forms.

Information We Collect
When you submit an inquiry, we collect the contact and project information you provide, such as name, company, email, phone, country, product interest, annual volume and message contents.

How We Use Information
We use submitted information to respond to your inquiry, evaluate technical requirements, prepare quotations, coordinate samples and provide engineering or commercial support.

Information Sharing
We do not sell inquiry information. We may share necessary details with internal sales, engineering, quality or production teams only for project evaluation and customer support.

Data Retention
Inquiry records may be retained for business follow-up, quotation history, compliance and project management. You may contact us to request correction or deletion where applicable.

Security
We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect inquiry information from unauthorized access, misuse or disclosure.

Contact
For privacy questions or requests, contact TEAO at info@teao-damper.com.
